Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Crust & Cost subscriptions, features, and privacy.

How does the 7-day free trial work?
When you download Crust & Cost, you get 7 full days of unrestricted access to all features—including unlimited recipe costing, inventory management, PDF export, and nutrition labels. You can cancel anytime directly in your Apple ID or Google Play account before the 7 days end without being charged.
Does the app work without an internet connection?
Yes! Crust & Cost stores your database directly on your device. You can create recipes, calculate costs, and check your pantry in the middle of a hot kitchen or bakery cellar without needing Wi-Fi or mobile data.
Can I convert between Metric and Imperial units?
Absolutely. Crust & Cost includes built-in conversions between grams, kilograms, milliliters, liters, ounces, pounds, and cups, with density support for dry cup ingredients like flour, cocoa powder, and sugar.
Are my secret recipes and client pricing safe?
Yes, 100%. We never sell, track, or share your proprietary recipes or pricing information. All data is stored on your local device and synced only to your private personal iCloud or Google Drive account.
